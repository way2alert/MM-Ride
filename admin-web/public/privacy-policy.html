Privacy Policy for Driver Partners

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MM Ride ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information gathered from driver partners who use the MM Ride – Driver mobile application.

Important Background Location Disclosure:
The MM Ride – Driver application accesses and tracks your device's geographical location in the foreground and background strictly while you are on an active duty shift. Location tracking begins when you tap "Start Duty" and ends automatically when you tap "End Duty".

1. Information We Collect

  • Precise Location Data: Real-time GPS coordinates, speed, and directional telemetry gathered during active duty shifts to power dispatch, route monitoring, depot proximity checks, and rider safety.
  • Personal Identification: Driver full name, mobile telephone number, driving licence (DL) number, Aadhaar number, residential address, and emergency contact details.
  • Identity Verification Documents: Live camera captures of your Driving Licence (front and back), Aadhaar Card (front and back), and a verification selfie.
  • Financial Information: UPI ID (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM) for daily 50% earnings split settlement payouts.
  • Vehicle & Inspection Telemetry: Vehicle handover condition photos, odometer readings, and fuel expense claims.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

  • To verify driver identity, driving eligibility, and RTO licence authenticity before assigning fleet vehicles.
  • To monitor active bike-taxi shifts, prevent rash driving/speeding, and assist drivers in emergency roadside situations (SOS).
  • To verify depot pickup and return geofences during bike check-in/check-out.
  • To calculate accurate gross revenue, daily fuel expense deductions, and process daily automated UPI payouts.

3. Data Storage & Security

All driver information and verification documents are securely stored using Google Cloud Platform and Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Encrypted Cloud Storage). All network communications are encrypted via TLS/HTTPS.

4. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted service providers including Google Firebase (Google LLC) for user authentication and backend database infrastructure. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to advertisers.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Driver records are retained for the duration of the driver partnership in accordance with commercial fleet regulations. Drivers may request account closure, suspension, and data deletion by contacting our Fleet Operations Support at support@mmride.com.
